Vita Plena

Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026 · Cognitive Christian

Vita Plena is a household app. What you put in it belongs to your household. This page says plainly what we collect, why, and what we never do.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture from Google. Used only to sign you in and to tell your household who you are.
  • Your household's data. Practices, tasks, events, notes, check-ins, reflections, and the other things you enter. Stored in Google Firestore under your household, readable only by members of that household.
  • Calendar events, only if you connect Google Calendar. We read events to show them alongside your rule of life. We do not write to your calendar without asking. The connection is per device and can be disconnected any time.
  • Messages to Beacon. When you speak or type to the companion, the message and a snapshot of your household's schedule are sent to Anthropic's Claude to produce a reply. Anthropic processes them under its own privacy terms and does not use them to train models. We keep a count of messages per day; we do not store the messages themselves on our servers.
  • Device settings such as bell sound and quiet hours stay on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers. There are no ads and no tracking pixels.
  • We do not read your reflections, examens, or check-ins. Reflections marked private are private to you even within your household.
  • We do not use your data to train any model.

Who can see what

Members of your household see the household's shared data. Private items (examens, private reflections, private notes) are visible only to the person who wrote them. Security rules on the database enforce this; a person outside your household cannot read it.

Your rights

  • Export. Settings → Export my household's data gives you everything as a file.
  • Delete. Settings → Delete my account removes your account and your user record. If you are the last member, the household and all its data are deleted with it. Deletion is immediate.
  • Questions. Write to support@cognitivechristian.com. We answer within two business days.

Children

Accounts are for adults. Children appear in the app as people without accounts, added by a parent, with only the name and details the parent chooses to enter.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
