Getting started

  • Sign in with your email and a password. One person creates the household and gets an invite code (Settings → Household); the other joins with it.
  • Look around first. The sign-in screen has a sample household you can explore without an account.
  • The rule of life is set up in four steps when you create a household, and can be changed any time from the menu → "Set up my rule again", or from Pray → The order of the day.

The bells

  • Each practice with a time rings on your phone at that hour, whether the app is open or not. Allow notifications when asked, or later in Settings → The bells.
  • Quiet hours, the sound, and "ring on this device" are per phone, so a tablet in the kitchen can ring out loud while a phone at work stays quiet.
  • If bells stop: check the phone's Settings → Notifications → Vita Plena, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb allows them.

Readings

The day's Mass readings are shown from the Douay-Rheims Bible (Challoner revision, public domain). The passages are the ones appointed in the current Roman lectionary for the United States. The official texts are at bible.usccb.org.

The family plan

  • A household starts with thirty days free. Afterwards the rule of life, the calendar, the prayers and the bells keep working; the plan keeps Beacon and the Sunday briefing going.
  • One plan covers every member of the household. Subscribe from Settings → Plan.
  • Restore a purchase after a new phone or a reinstall: Settings → Plan → Restore a purchase.
  • Cancel in your Apple ID or Google account subscription settings. Access continues to the end of the paid period.

Your data

  • Export everything as a file: Settings → Your data → Export.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Your data → Delete my account. If you are the last member, the household and all its data go with it, immediately.
  • Calendar events pulled from Google Calendar are copies; disconnecting stops the pull.

Something is wrong

  • Sign out and back in (menu → Sign out).
  • Web: Settings → "Refresh the app" clears the offline copy.
  • Still stuck? Email us with what you saw.
